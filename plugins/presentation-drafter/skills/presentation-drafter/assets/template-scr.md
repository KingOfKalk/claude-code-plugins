### Situation (10–15% of content)

_What does everyone already agree on? Only noncontroversial facts._

### Complication (15–20% of content)

_Why can't we just keep doing what we're doing? What creates urgency?_

### Resolution (60–70% of content)

_What do we do about it? Specific: what, how, who, when._

## Winston Star Check (for important talks)

- [ ] **Symbol** — Is there a visual icon people associate with this?
- [ ] **Slogan** — Can someone describe my idea in one phrase?
- [ ] **Surprise** — What defies expectations?
- [ ] **Salient Idea** — What single concept sticks out?
- [ ] **Story** — Can I narrate how/why this matters?

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Empowerment promise in first 2 minutes?

- [ ] Slides are mostly images, ≥40pt text, zero bullets?
- [ ] Key messages alone tell the full story (horizontal logic)?

- [ ] Final slide = Contributions (not "Questions?" or "Thank you")?
- [ ] Practiced with someone unfamiliar with the topic?
